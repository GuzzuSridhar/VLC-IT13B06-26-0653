Using the Oracle HR schema, write SQL queries to answer the following questions.
1. Display the employee name and their department name.
2. Display the employee name, job title, and salary of each employee.
3. Display the employee name and the city where their department is located.
4. Display all employees, including those who are not assigned to any department, along with their department names.
5. Display all departments, including those that have no employees, along with the employee names.
6. Display each employee's name along with their manager's name.
7. Display the employee name, department name, and country name where the department is located.
8. Display employees who work in the same department as employee 'Steven King'.
9. Display pairs of employees who work in the same department, excluding employees paired with themselves.
10. Display the employee name, job title, department name, and department location for employees whose salary is greater than 10,000.
